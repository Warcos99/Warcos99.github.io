Food Assistance

Times are tough, and there have been several situation where I have needed food assistance. I know you have too. But asking for help from friends and family can be embarasing and a barrier to actuallly making use of resources within our community. Times WILL get tougher, so let's solve this problem!

A succesful mutual aid food program, in my opinion, needs to satisfy a couple conditions, lest the program be short lived.

  • It should not necessitate that people in need ask for help.
  • It needs to be a reliable program that we can count on will exist when we need it.
  • Coordination and planning for the program must be simple and open to the public eye.
  • There should be no barrier on who within the community is allowed to make use of the program.
  • The burden of the supplies and labor for the running of the program should fall on everyone in the community as a whole, not on a single person.

With all that in mind, based on some feedback and ideas from community members, we've come up with the idea of a weekly potluck. I am dubbing it Project: Hestia in honor of the Greek God.

Project: Hestia

Join us for a weekly potluck, organized through the BlackBox Discord page. Each week will be hosted by a different community volunteer! Planned food and all notable ingredients will be explicitely noted down on that same Discord channel. We will eat, be merry, discuss politics, philosphy, love, life, and everything else!

Once the ingredient list is published, if there are any dietary conflicts, Let us know as soon as you can, and we will strive to accomodate as best as we can! You can notify us through Discord, or if you wish to do so anoynmously, you can fill out the form below!

Everyone is welcome to come no matter what. You want to come hang out with friends on a weekly basis and talk about life? Come join us! You want to come get some food and take some leftovers home because you are hungry? Come Join us! You need an excuse to leave the house and touch some grass? COME JOIN US!

Potlucks will be happening every Sunday from the hours of 5pm till 8pm.

If a communication breakdown ever happens (internet outage, power outage, or any other sort of situation that makes it hard to communicate and coordiante) the weekly potluck will be at my place and will feature a simple "Beans and Rice" dish by default.

This is a BlackBox Community project (i.e. Not and official BlackBox Arts Collective program organized through the proper burocratic channels). The note here is not just a semantics thing, it is here to highlight that this project is something I'm kinda just doing, and is not necessarily representative of the whole community. It is just some way in which I am trying to contribute my people.

Food Assistance Request

Need more explicit food assistance? This form can be anonymous if you want. Just fill in the "name" field with a fake name (like "anon"), fill in the "message" field with details regarding your food and diet situation, and that message will go to a special mailbox where I can see and process food assitance requests. Once a request comes in, we will use the BlackBox Discord to coordinate a covert food pickup for you, or set up an impromptu potluck event.
