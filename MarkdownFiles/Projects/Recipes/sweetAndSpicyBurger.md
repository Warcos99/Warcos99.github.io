# Sweet and Spicy Bacon Gouda Burger

## Ingredients

- Potato Buns
- Garlic Powder
- Bacon
- Honey Dijon mustard
- Sour Cream
- Ground Beef
- Sliced Gouda
- Crispy Fried Onions
- Brown Sugar
- Hot Sauce

---

## Recipe

- Bacon
	- Mix Brown Sugar (2tbsp) and hot sauce (2tsp)
	- Coat bacon in mix and bake (450 for 10 minutes)
- Make the following mix:
	- Honey Dijon mustard, mayo
- Make the following mix:
	- Sour cream, mayo, hot sauce, water
	- until "drizzling" consistency
	- add salt and pepper
- Make Patty
	- Beef, salt, pepper
	- cook until desired done-ness
	- Gouda cheese at the end
- Assemble
