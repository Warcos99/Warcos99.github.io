# Griddled Onion Burger

## Ingredients

- Garlic
- Yellow Onion
- Potato Buns
- Dijon Mustard
- Mayo
- Ground Beef
- Salt & Pepper

---

## Recipe

- dice half onion. slice the other half
- Make Aeloi
  - Combine: Mustard, Mayo, Garlic, Salt & Pepper
- Make Patty
  - Combine: Beef, Salt, Pepper
  - Cook to desired done-ness
- Onion Rounds
  - Cook diced onions with salt & pepper
  - mix diced onions into aeoli
  - cook sliced onions into onion rounds
- Toast Buns with garlic and butter
- Assemble
