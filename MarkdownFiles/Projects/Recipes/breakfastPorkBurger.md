# Breakfast Pork Sausage and Egg Burger

## Ingredients

- Garlic Powder
- Potato Buns
- Ground Pork
- Dried Thyme
- Dried Rosemary
- Shredded Cheddar
- Brown Sugar
- eggs

---

## Recipe

- Patty
	- combine pork + garlic powder + thyme + rosemary + brown sugar +  salt + pepper
	- form pork balls
	- on medium high skillet, add pork ball
	- squish ball with spatula
	- add cheese at the end
- Cook eggs to preference
- toast buns
- assemble (add hot sauce)
