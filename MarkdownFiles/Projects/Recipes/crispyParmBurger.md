# Crispy Parm Frico Burger

## Ingredients

- Yellow Onion
- Potato Buns
- Ground Beef
- Soy Sauce
- Shredded Parm Cheese
- Garlic

---

## Recipe

- Preheat oven to 450
- Parm Frico
	- on oven sheet, place parm into little piles with the width of a burger bun
	- bake until cheese is melted and crispy at edges
- Caramelize Onions
	- onion, soy sauce, sugar, water
- Pattys
	- Beef + salt + pepper
	- cook until desired done-ness
- Toast buns
	- with garlic and butter
- Assemble
