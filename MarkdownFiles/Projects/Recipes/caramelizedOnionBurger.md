# Caramelized Onion Burger

## Ingredients

- Yellow Onion
- Garlic
- Potato Buns
- Ground Beef
- Mayo
- Wasabi
- Salt and Pepper
- Sugar
- Butter
- Veg Oil

---

## Recipe

- Peel and thinly slice onion
- Peel and finely chop garlic
- In large pan over medium high heat
	- Heat oil, add onion, add salt and pepper
	- cook until softened and browned
	- add 1tsp sugar to 1/4 water
	- add sugar water to onion
	- Caramelize onion, transfer to a bowl
- Form Beef into patties, season with salt and pepper
- In a small bowl, combine:
	- mayo
	- garlic
	- wasabi
	- salt and pepper
- Cook patties and toast buns
- Assemble
